# Kids

Vibe-coded minigames made together with my kids.

This repository is a home for small games and creative experiments we build together.

**Elf** was created by Maja (age 9) and Jula (age 7). Their black cat Lucek helps collect items. Falling into a pit or touching a hazard costs him one life; every cat pickup restores all seven lives and brings him back if needed.

## Getting started

Open [Elf](elf/elf.html) in a modern browser to play. The game works offline and needs no installation.

When served from HTTPS or `localhost`, Elf can also be installed as a PWA. After the first online visit, its files are cached for offline play.

Move with **A / D** or **← / →**, jump with **Space**, **W**, or **↑**, and attack with **X** or **J**. On touch screens, use the on-screen controls. Press **P** or **Esc** to pause, **M** to toggle sound, and **F** for full screen.
